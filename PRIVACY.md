# SmartSeek privacy policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

SmartSeek saves YouTube playback progress so you can resume videos. It has no developer-run backend, analytics, advertising, account system, or device sync. Your saved library is not automatically sent to the developer or sold.

## Data stored on your device

SmartSeek reads video information on supported YouTube watch pages and stores video IDs, titles, playback positions, durations, update times, music/completion flags, favorites, manual-save choices, and preferences in your Chrome profile using `chrome.storage.local`. Upgrade backups and pending recovery operations are also stored locally.

Playback sessions, import previews, and temporary undo copies use `chrome.storage.session`. SmartSeek does not request Chrome's browsing-history permission or monitor activity on other websites.

## External connections

Video thumbnails load from YouTube's image service at `i.ytimg.com`. These requests include the video ID and expose ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, to that service.

Feedback links open Google Forms, and the support link opens Buy Me a Coffee. Uninstalling may o◊pen an optional Google Forms feedback page. Information you choose to submit is received by the form recipient or service provider and is subject to that provider's privacy practices.

## Reports and exported files

Issue reports are prepared locally and include extension/browser versions, preferences, storage health, player state, and descriptions you enter. Automatic diagnostics exclude saved video titles, IDs, URLs, and watch history. Reports are not submitted automatically; you can review, copy, or download them before choosing to share them.

Backup and recovery exports contain library data. Imports are processed locally. Keep exported files private and delete them separately when no longer needed.

## Retention and deletion

Auto-delete is off by default. If enabled, it removes non-favorite saves after 30, 60, or 90 days without a progress update. Favorites are excluded.

You can remove individual videos or use **Clear saved videos** in settings. Upgrade recovery copies may retain previously removed videos until you clear the saved library. Clearing the library removes these copies but keeps preferences. Exported files are unaffected.

Uninstalling clears Chrome's local extension storage. See [Chrome's storage documentation](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/reference/api/storage).
